Enrolment
How does enrolment work and when do we recommend it?
1 min read

Enrolment is simple: 1) book a free placement test online or by phone, 2) we recommend a level and a timetable, 3) you pay the course fee and receive your welcome pack by email.

We recommend enrolling at least one week before the course starts, as evening groups at the City Centre school fill up quickly.

Not sure which course is right for you? Book a free placement test and we will recommend a level and a timetable.
