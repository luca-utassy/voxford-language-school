Living in Manchester
Moving to Manchester? Get your English job-ready
1 min read

Relocating is one of the most stressful things you can do. New flat, new job, new school for the kids, and on top of everything a language that sounds different from the one you learned at school. Manchester English has its own rhythm, and many of our students tell us the first weeks were the hardest.

Our General English evening course is built for people who have just arrived. Classes are small, the topics are practical (renting, the GP, job interviews, small talk at work) and you can start any Monday after a free placement test.

Most students who start at B1 level reach a comfortable B2 within two terms. If you need English for a specific job, ask about our Business English and ESOL for Work options at the Salford school.

Not sure which course is right for you? Book a free placement test and we will recommend a level and a timetable.
